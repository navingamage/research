I'm the tech lead for a small payments platform team, and it's time for half-year reviews. I need to decide a performance rating for each of my four engineers and give each of them one clear goal for the next half. In Google Sheets I'll go through our rating guide and each person's numbers for April to September, like commitments met, rework, incidents and code reviews. In Google Docs I'll read the peer feedback and my 1:1 notes and highlight what actually matters. Then back in Sheets I'll score each engineer on delivery, quality, teamwork and growth, pick a final rating, and check the team spread against the guide. Last, I'll write a short review summary in Google Docs with each person's rating, the reason, and their goal for next half. Deliverables are the ratings workbook and the review summary as a PDF.
