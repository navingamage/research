# Studio idea text (paste into "Describe your idea")

**Domain / Role:** Software engineering. Tech lead / engineering manager for a team of four engineers.

**Goal:** I'm the tech lead for a small payments platform team, and it's time for half-year reviews. I need to decide a performance rating for each of my four engineers and give each of them one clear goal for the next half. In Google Sheets I'll go through our rating guide and each person's numbers for April to September, like commitments met, rework, incidents and code reviews. In Google Docs I'll read the peer feedback and my 1:1 notes and highlight what actually matters. Then back in Sheets I'll score each engineer on delivery, quality, teamwork and growth, pick a final rating, and check the team spread against the guide. Last, I'll write a short review summary in Google Docs with each person's rating, the reason, and their goal for next half. Deliverables are the ratings workbook and the review summary as a PDF.

**Starting materials:** Using existing material. A workbook with our rating guide, the team's numbers for the half and a blank ratings tab, a doc with peer feedback and my 1:1 notes, and a blank review summary template. All AI-generated before recording. The people are made up.

**Steps (with the apps for each):**
- Google Sheets: read the rating guide (scale, category weights, what each level should look like, how many people can be rated high) and go through each engineer's numbers.
- Google Docs: read the peer feedback and 1:1 notes and highlight the points that change the picture.
- Google Sheets: score each engineer on delivery, quality, teamwork and growth, look at the weighted score, decide the final rating, and check the team spread is within the guide.
- Google Docs: fill in the review summary with each person's rating, the reason and one goal for next half, then download it as a PDF.

**Deliverable:** The ratings workbook (.xlsx) with category scores, final ratings and the spread check, and the review summary PDF with a rating, a reason and a next-half goal for each engineer. A reviewer can check that each rating lines up with the numbers and feedback and stays within the rating guide.

**Apps:** Google Sheets, Google Docs
